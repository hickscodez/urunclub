Why U run?

The consistency behind the Log

In 10+ years of running I have never taken more than 1 week off. Through college seasons, 125-mile build weeks, and everything since Boston, I've learned how to hold high volume and high performance while keeping my body and mind healthy the whole way. That balance has carried me to top 10 finishes at the Marathon Majors and 2 marathons under 2:10.

That consistency is what's kept me in love with the sport and I want to share it with as many people as possible! My training data isn't a set target. It's a constant to measure your own week against, at your percentage, toward your goals.

We can't spell running without U!

Pick the tier at or below your current weekly volume. The challenge is consistency against a moving number, not a volume jump. Not medical advice. You know your body and your goal. I only know my mileage.
